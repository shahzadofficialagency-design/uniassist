Abbasia Campus
A Historical building was initially constructed by His Highness Nawab Sir Sadiq Muhammad Khan Abbasi – V in the heart of the city near Government Sadiq Egerton College at University Chowk, Bahawalpur, to impart higher education in 1950.
VC
Vice Chancellor Secretariat
Main administrative office of the university located at the historical campus.
GH
Ghulam Muhammad Ghotvi Hall
A historical hall situated within the campus used for university events and activities.
FL
Faculty of Law
Academic department providing legal education and professional law programs.
BK
Banks & Admission Cell
Campus banking facilities and admission support services for students.
RO
Registrar Office
Handles academic records, registrations, and official university documentation.
EX
Examination Division
Responsible for examination management, results, and student assessments.
