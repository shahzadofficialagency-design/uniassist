Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus
Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus (BJC), also known as Main Campus or BJ Campus, is located at Hasilpur Road, about eight kilometers from the city center. Ten out of thirteen faculties are established at BJ Campus.
VC
Vice Chancellor Secretariat
Main administrative secretariat located within Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus.
SC
Sports Complex
Includes cricket ground, football ground, squash court and other sports facilities.
LB
Libraries & Computer Labs
Modern libraries and computer laboratories supporting academic research and learning.
MA
Main Auditorium
Central venue for conferences, seminars, cultural and academic events.
GM
Grand Jamia Masjid
A large mosque facility serving students, faculty and campus staff.
MC
Medical Center
Healthcare and emergency medical services available for students and staff.
SH
Student Hostels
Residential hostel facilities for male and female students within the campus.
SP
Solar Park & Farms
Includes solar park, flower farms, vegetable farms, fruit orchards and fish pond.
IT
Directorate of IT
Directorate of Information Technology managing digital and technical services.
AG
Faculty of Agriculture
Faculty offering agricultural sciences and research-based academic programs.
ET
Faculty of Engineering
Engineering and technology programs with modern labs and research facilities.
BM
Business Management Institute
Institute of Business Management and Administrative Sciences for commerce studies.
The Islamia University of Bahawalpur
The Islamia University of Bahawalpur was established in the year 1975 by upgrading historical institute of the Jamia Abbasia.
