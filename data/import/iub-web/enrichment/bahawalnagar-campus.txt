Bahawalnagar Campus
The Islamia University of Bahawalpur established Bahawalnagar Sub-Campus in 2005. The campus is located on Minchanabad Road, Bahawalnagar, scattered over 50 acres of land. Currently, there are 22 teaching departments offering more than 40 undergraduate and post graduate programmes in the field of business management, natural and social sciences, arts and languages, and information and technology.
