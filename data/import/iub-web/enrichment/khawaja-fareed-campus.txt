Khawaja Fareed Campus
Khawaja Fareed Campus was also constructed by His Highness Nawab Sir Sadiq Muhammad Khan Abbasi-V in 1925, near Abbasia High School on Railway Road. Currently, the Faculty of Pharmacy and the Faculty of Medicine and Allied Health Sciences are functional there.
