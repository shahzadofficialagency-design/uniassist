Residential and Hostel Facilities
EC
Employees Residential Colony
Residential colony at Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus facilitating more than 300 university employee families.
FH
Faculty & Executive Hostels
Separate bachelor hostels, executive hostels and foreign faculty hostels for faculty officers and guests.
HS
Student Hostel System
Sixteen hostels across five campuses providing accommodation facility for 10,000 students.
AC
Abbasia Campus Hostels
Rabia Hall for girls and Abu Bakar Hall for boys are located at Abbasia Campus.
GH
Girls Hostels
Multiple girls hostels including Fatima-tuz-Zahra, Ayesha Siddiqa, Zainab and Halima Halls.
BH
Boys Hostels
Boys hostels at Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus include Umar Hall, Ali Hall, Ahmad Hall and Usman Hall.
BN
Bahawalnagar Campus Hostels
Ayesha Hall for girls and Abu Bakar Hall for boys provide boarding facilities in Bahawalnagar Campus.
RK
Rahim Yar Khan Hostels
Ayesha Hall for girls and Iqbal Hall for boys offer hostel accommodation in Rahim Yar Khan Campus.
CF
Cafeteria & Dining Facilities
Every hostel includes cafeteria, dining hall and common rooms for students comfort.
CL
Computer Labs & Libraries
Hostels are equipped with modern computer labs, reading rooms and library facilities.
SG
Sports & Gym Facilities
Indoor games, sports clubs, gym facilities and lush green gardens are available in hostels.
SP
Study Parks
Beautiful study parks with flowers, lakes, sports courts, internet and separate sitting areas.
