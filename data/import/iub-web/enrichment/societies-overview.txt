About Student Societies
The student societies were formed under the umbrella of Directorate of Students Affairs under the direction of the Worthy Vice Chancellor. These societies provide opportunities for students to develop soft skills, social intelligence and leadership qualities while participating in co-curricular activities and university events.
01
Leadership Development
Students learn teamwork, management and event organization through practical activities.
02
Co-Curricular Activities
Various academic, sports and cultural activities are organized throughout the year.
03
Student Recognition
Students receive certificates, awards and recognition for outstanding performance.
