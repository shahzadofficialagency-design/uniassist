University Transport
The Islamia University of Bahawalpur is providing transport services to its students, faculty, staff and departmental guests. This transport fleet is one of the largest fleets owned by any educational institute in the country. The University is providing safe, reliable and efficient transport service to students, faculty and staff in following areas
Campus to Campus Facility
Study Tours
Field Trips
Emergency Services
Pick and Drop Facility for Special Students
Service in Sports & Co-Curricular Activities
The Islamia University of Bahawalpur also provides transport facility to students and employees living in nearby towns of Ahmedpur, Khairpur Tamai Wali, Khanqah Sharif, Lal Sohanra, Lodhran and Yazman.
