Vision & Mission
Our Vision
To be a world-class university with a global outlook and be a catalyst for socioeconomic transformation of the region.
Our Mission
To produce human and intellectual capital of high socioeconomic value through top quality academic and research programs, fostering an environment conducive to teaching, learning, innovation, service, and community engagement.
